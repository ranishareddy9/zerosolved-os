Overview
The whole case on one page.
General contractors and subcontractors suffer significant financial losses due to uncompensated weather delays, a problem that persists because current documentation methods are manual, inconsistent, and easily disputed. This leads to prolonged payment cycles and disputes, impacting cash flow for small and medium-sized businesses. Without a standardized, verifiable system, weather impact remains a source of friction and financial strain.
researcher note · [modeled]
A platform that automatically logs, verifies, and quantifies weather delays will reduce disputes and accelerate payments for construction projects.
