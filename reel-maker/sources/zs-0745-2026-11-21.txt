Overview
The whole case on one page.
Creative professionals and skilled service providers struggle to effectively showcase their process and the value they deliver beyond a final output. Potential clients find it difficult to assess the depth of expertise and problem-solving skills involved, leading to selection based on superficial criteria. This lack of transparency in process results in misaligned expectations and missed opportunities for high-quality collaborations.
researcher note · [modeled]
A platform that facilitates the creation and sharing of 'proof-of-process' portfolios will enable service professionals to attract higher-value clients by transparently demonstrating their problem-solving methodologies.
The case in 30 seconds
who bleeds Freelance designers, developers, and consultants (ages 28-45) who rely on project-based work and find it hard to articulate their unique value proposition beyond a polished final product.
what it costs $4,500 — average lost deal value due to client uncertainty about process
why now The increasing commoditization of creative services online necessitates a new way for professionals to differentiate themselves based on their expertise and methodology.
reachable market $600m — Annual TAM for freelance creative and consulting services in North America
