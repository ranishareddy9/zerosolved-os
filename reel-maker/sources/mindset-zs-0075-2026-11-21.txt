Overview
The whole case on one page.
Creative and digital agencies lose up to a quarter of billable capacity to unstructured client feedback cycles that breach contract scope. Account leads and designers absorb out-of-scope iteration rounds rather than trigger billing disputes, causing margin collapse on fixed-fee and retainer engagements.
researcher note · [modeled]
If creative review software enforces scope constraints natively through automated contract-linked change tracking, agency margin leakage from unbilled revisions drops by at least 45%. Downgrade if agencies prefer absorbing scope creep over risking client churn during feedback gates.
The case in 30 seconds
who bleeds Account directors and lead designers at 5-50 person digital, branding, and content agencies managing fixed-scope client retainers.
the bleed annual margin drained per agency pod in unbilled revision churn
the gap Existing proofing platforms aggregate visual comments but treat every piece of feedback as valid, lacking contract-aware scope boundaries or automated delta-costing.
the wedge A browser extension and client-facing review portal that tags client comments against the initial brief and generates instant out-of-scope cost deltas before submission.
the endgame The core client collaboration infrastructure for professional services, dynamically linking project briefs, approval states, and invoice adjustments.
the window 18-24 months, before primary project management suites embed automated change-order generation directly into markup views.
