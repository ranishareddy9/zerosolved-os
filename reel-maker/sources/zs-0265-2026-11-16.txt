Overview
The whole case on one page.
Widespread adoption of low-latency LLM interview copilots and undetectable screen overlays has compromised remote engineering hiring funnels. Engineering leaders now waste senior staff hours evaluating candidates who silently proxy answers in real time, driving up 90-day probation terminations and forcing retrograde in-person rounds.
researcher note · [modeled]
Remote hiring integrity cannot be solved by client-side browser lockdown extensions; downgrading occurs if enterprise video platforms natively embed hardware-level eye and screen verification by default.
The case in 30 seconds
who bleeds Engineering hiring managers and talent acquisition leads hiring remote mid-to-senior software engineers in North America and Western Europe.
the bleed annual avoidable cost per 10-person engineering team hiring remotely
the gap Existing proctoring tools rely on invasive screen permissions and eye-tracking that candidates bypass with secondary devices, while live code environments evaluate regurgitation rather than dynamic problem breakdown.
the wedge A 20-minute live, interactive architecture refactoring sandbox that assesses unpromptable real-time system debugging and latency patterns.
the endgame The default verification protocol for live high-stakes professional assessments, replacing traditional video-and-takehome screening stacks.
the window 18-24 months before operating systems and meeting platforms build native tamper-detection layers.
