Overview
The whole case on one page.
Manufacturing plant operators suffer from unaccounted downtime because maintenance logs are filled with generic 'miscellaneous' entries. This lack of specificity prevents root cause identification and targeted improvement efforts. Consequently, recurring issues plague production lines, leading to lost output and increased operational costs.

A data-driven platform that automatically categorizes and analyzes manufacturing downtime based on sensor data and operator input will reduce unplanned downtime by 15% within two years.
