Overview
The whole case on one page.
Large language models and semantic matching algorithms embedded in applicant tracking systems reject up to 75% of qualified applicants without auditability. Talent acquisition teams face increasing legal exposure from bias and disparate impact while losing high-calibre candidates to uncalibrated semantic filters. Enterprise recruiting leaders have no native mechanism to stress-test their scoring pipelines against simulated candidate variance.
researcher note · [modeled]
In-house compliance and recruiting operations teams will pay for automated counterfactual bias auditing and calibration software for ATS screening engines before regulatory penalties bite; file downgrades if enterprise ATS vendors ship verified, transparent algorithmic interpretability dashboards natively.
The case in 30 seconds
who bleeds Enterprise talent acquisition operations leads, corporate recruiting directors, and HR compliance officers at companies hiring 500+ staff annually.
the bleed annual direct sourcing waste and agency backfill costs per 1,000 corporate hires
the gap ATS platforms rank or filter resumes using opaque embeddings, whereas recruiting teams need continuous counterfactual audits showing exactly why specific candidate profiles were rejected.
the wedge A standalone batch-file audit tool that runs synthetic resume permutations through an employer's ATS configuration to identify false-negative rejection triggers.
the endgame The definitive compliance and calibration clearinghouse for automated employment decision tools across global enterprise HR pipelines.
the window 18-36 months, driven by enforcement of NYC Local Law 144, EU AI Act employment provisions, and EEOC guidance.
