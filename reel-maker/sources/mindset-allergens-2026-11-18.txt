Overview
The whole case on one page.
Independent restaurants and bistros rotating menus daily or weekly struggle to maintain compliant allergen matrices across ingredient substitutions. When suppliers swap wholesale brands without notice, updating manual allergen matrices before service routinely fails, exposing venues to severe legal liability and guest safety risks.
researcher note · [modeled]
A zero-admin ingredient-to-allergen parser integrated into kitchen prep notes eliminates compliance friction; downgraded if independent operators accept spreadsheet audits as legally sufficient without dedicated tooling.
The case in 30 seconds
who bleeds Head chefs and general managers at independent single-site and small-group (2-8 sites) hospitality venues with rotating weekly menus.
the bleed Direct unrecovered labour and comped table costs per venue from manual allergen administration
the gap Enterprise inventory ERPs demand exhaustive recipe itemisation unsuitable for daily specials, while digital QR menus rely on static manual checkboxes that lag kitchen prep reality.
the wedge A daily WhatsApp-to-print allergen sheet generator that turns a photo or text of the daily prep board into an instant FOH matrix and POS-synced allergen table in 30 seconds.
the endgame The real-time food composition data layer connecting wholesale supplier invoice line items directly to point-of-sale allergen disclosures across independent hospitality.
the window 18-24 months, driven by statutory enforcement of allergen labelling laws (e.g. Natasha's Law, Owen's Law proposals, FASTER Act) and hospitality labour shortages.
