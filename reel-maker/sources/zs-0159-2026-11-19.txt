Overview
The whole case on one page.
First-generation and regional university undergraduates are systematically filtered out of high-trajectory professional internships due to geographic relocation costs, opaque recruiting timelines, and uncompensated credentialing tasks. Legacy campus recruitment platforms index on institutional prestige and pedigree filters, leaving qualified non-target students locked out of early career pipelines.
researcher note · [modeled]
A skill-verified micro-internship marketplace funded by mid-tier corporate talent acquisition budgets can disintermediate traditional target-school campus recruitment; invalidated if enterprise hiring managers refuse to interview candidates without elite university provenance or if student completion rates for remote micro-projects fall below 40%.
The case in 30 seconds
who bleeds Undergraduate students at non-Russell Group / non-target state universities studying quantitative or professional disciplines without family financial backstops for metropolitan living costs.
the bleed Average economic penalty per non-target undergraduate attempting to secure a Tier-1 summer internship
the gap Existing job boards aggregate open listings and resume parsers filter by university brand, while missing verified work-sample validation that proves execution ability independent of institutional pedigree.
the wedge Paid 15-hour remote project sprints evaluated directly by engineering and commercial hiring managers at growth-stage technology firms.
the endgame The primary infrastructure for early career talent discovery and verified competence accreditation, replacing university career fairs and campus OCR programs.
the window 18-24 months, as enterprise return-to-office mandates collide with DEI budget contractions and rising entry-level ATS rejection rates driven by generative AI application spam.
