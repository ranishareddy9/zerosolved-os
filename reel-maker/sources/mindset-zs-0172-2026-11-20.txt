Overview
The whole case on one page.
Independent off-premise caterers take 48 to 72 hours to return bespoke event proposals because pricing requires manual cross-checking of fluctuating ingredient wholesale costs, dynamic staffing ratios, and kitchen prep schedules. Over 40% of qualified event leads book a competitor before receiving the initial itemised proposal. This operational delay converts high-intent, high-margin private and corporate dining enquiries into unrecoverable administrative overhead.
researcher note · [modeled]
A dynamic, recipe-costed proposal engine with automated labour-tiering can compress bespoke catering quote turnaround from 72 hours to under 10 minutes, doubling proposal conversion; downgraded if caterers refuse to standardise baseline ingredient formulas.
The case in 30 seconds
who bleeds Owner-operators and sales managers at independent catering firms handling 60 to 300 bespoke events annually (£400k to £3M gross revenue).
the bleed Annual lost coordinator administrative payroll per sales desk, excluding lost deposit revenue
the gap Generic event management tools lack kitchen-aware recipe costing and live supplier indexation, while legacy catering ERPs feature rigid desktop UIs that cannot generate interactive, self-adjusting client proposals.
the wedge A web-based dynamic proposal generator for 20-200 guest private dining and corporate lunches that auto-calculates margin-safe menus and produces instant signable quotes.
the endgame The vertical financial operating system and automated wholesale replenishment layer for the independent off-premise catering market.
the window 18 to 24 months, accelerated by hospitality staff shortages and corporate clients expecting instant consumer-grade checkout workflows.
