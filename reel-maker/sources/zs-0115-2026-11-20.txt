Overview
The whole case on one page.
Commercial invoice finance providers obscure true capital costs behind floating discount tiers, misapplied collection float, and complex aging surcharges. Small business owners bleed thousands each quarter in undisclosed facility leakage without tooling to audit statements against master service agreements.
researcher note · [modeled]
B2B operators financing >£1.5M in receivables annually will pay £199–£499/month for automated factor statement reconciliation; file downgrades if factoring lenders adopt open ledger APIs and standardised fee disclosures.
The case in 30 seconds
who bleeds Owner-managers and finance heads at £1M–£8M turnover firms in haulage, temporary staffing, and specialist subcontracting.
the bleed annual unbudgeted factoring leakage and forensic reconciliation labour per operator
the gap Factoring brokers compare headline rates only at origination; cloud accounting packages log net bank deposits without auditing lender PDF fee schedules against signed facility agreements.
the wedge A PDF and CSV statement parser that checks monthly factoring statements against contract fee rules and outputs line-item overcharge claim notices.
the endgame An automated multi-facility liquidity router that dynamically allocates ledger tranches to the lowest net APR factoring provider in real time.
the window 24-36 months, as legacy factoring houses delay digital reporting while digital neo-factors steadily capture new company formations.
