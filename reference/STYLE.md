# Reference style: minimal mindset reels and posts

The owner's reference pieces (2 reels, 2 image posts) are saved in this folder **on the owner's computer only**. They belong to another creator, so they are git-ignored and must never be committed, re-posted or copied. This file describes what to borrow from them; it is the reference the cloud runs use.

## What the reference does
- **Reel 1 (about 18 s):** two pictogram figures side by side on black, each with a small uppercase mono label. One keeps re-laying the same first bricks, the other builds a full wall. No narration, no numbers, one idea.
- **Reel 2 (about 16 s):** the same few minutes spent two ways, shown as two rows of small scenes with uppercase labels, then one line of text. Ends on the account's logo.
- **Post 1:** one short sentence, centred on black, with one word styled for emphasis.
- **Post 2:** a word-and-symbol equation with tiny uppercase labels under each term.
- Music is a popular licensed song added in the Instagram app.

## What we borrow
- Pure black, lots of empty space, white pictogram figures, one comparison per reel.
- Tiny uppercase mono labels; one short line of text at a time.
- About 15–18 s per reel; posts with a single sentence or equation.
- Calm motion: figures work, think, sit; things stack or crumble. No flashy effects.

## What we never copy
- Their concepts, wording, characters, logo, handle or watermark. Every comparison and line is written fresh for Zerosolved's audience (founders, students, side-builders).
- Their music. Instagram's licensed songs can't be added through Metricool, so every reel uses the generated royalty-free track (`reel-maker/lib/music.mjs`).

## How it becomes Zerosolved
- Theme: builder mindset, always tied to "Stop guessing what to build." Contrasts like guesser vs solver, idea collector vs problem hunter.
- Brand: Sora / Inter / JetBrains Mono, lime `#88AE1B` for 1–3 key words (opportunity), orange for the struggling side, small wordmark top-left and "971 case files" top-right.
- Every reel ends with the end card: zerosolved wordmark, "Stop guessing. Start solving.", "Explore Zerosolved.com →".
- No digits anywhere in mindset pieces: there is no case file behind them, so the numbers rule means no numbers.
